Our Story

Baghdad, Let's Rise

Nobody knows exactly who he is. Some say he ran a spice route between Beirut and Basra. Others say he's an old soul chasing the perfect bite. One thing is certain: Don Chicko is Baghdadi — and he lives for chicken.

Not the ordinary kind. The kind you bite once and never forget. Crispy or grilled, skin or center — he wanted it all to hit right. So he did what every good chef does: he built a fire. Not just any fire — he lit it with four flames.

4 Friends.
One flame.

The Flames

Everything we do comes from one of these four

Flame of Passion

Chicken done right — juicy, crispy, unforgettable. Marinated to the center, not just the skin.

Flame of Precision

Care. Timing. Temperature. Sequence. Good chicken can't save bad execution.

Flame of Togetherness

No vision comes to life alone. Dishes made to be shared, at a table that feels like home.

Flame of Character

The Baghdadi spirit — bold, rooted, unmistakable. When you have your own flavor, you don't need to shout.
