Our Story

Baghdad, Let's Rise

Nobody knows exactly who he is. Some say he ran a spice route between Beirut and Basra. Others say he's an old soul chasing the perfect bite. One thing is certain: Don Chicko is Baghdadi — and he lives for chicken.

Not the ordinary kind. The kind you bite once and never forget. Crispy or grilled, skin or center — he wanted it all to hit right. So he did what every good chef does: he built a fire. Not just any fire — he lit it with four flames.

4 Friends.
